04 / THE HANDS BEHIND THE IDEA

A family.
A craft.
Since 1855.

Seven generations of working with wood.

The workshop’s story began with Sebastiano Turchetti and his son Francesco. Today, Manifattura Ravennate continues that family tradition in Ravenna.

From doors and windows to custom furniture and design pieces, the work combines knowledge of timber with contemporary production techniques.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Behind
the idea.

Who makes this hidden bookshelf door?+

The product shown in the supplied photograph is a realization by Manifattura Ravennate. This page lets you explore its composition and concealed passage.

Can I discuss a bespoke project?+

Yes. The workshop produces custom doors, furniture, and design pieces. Contact the team with your space, drawings, or initial idea to discuss feasibility and a quotation.

What about dimensions, finishes, and installation?+

These are agreed for the individual project. The photograph and digital scene are visual references, not technical specifications. Ask the workshop about materials, measurements, installation, and delivery.

Where can I find the workshop?+

Via Baiona, 53, 48123 Ravenna, Italy. Call +39 0544 451651 or email m.ravennate@gmail.com to arrange your visit.
